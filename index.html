Everything you need to know about how a Mofis managed office works.

MOFIS is a managed office solutions company that delivers fully customized, plug-and-play workspaces for businesses of all sizes. From sourcing the right commercial space to designing, building, furnishing, and managing your office, we provide an end-to-end workplace solution that allows you to move in and begin operations without the complexities of setting up and managing an office yourself.

At MOFIS, we go beyond providing office space. We create workplaces that are tailored to your business, brand, and operational needs. Unlike conventional managed office providers that offer standardized layouts, MOFIS delivers customized office environments, premium design, enterprise-grade infrastructure, and dedicated facility management — all under one roof. Our focus is on creating workspaces that grow with your business while ensuring a seamless workplace experience every day.

Absolutely. Every business has its own identity, culture, and way of working. That's why every MOFIS workspace is designed around your specific requirements. From branding and space planning to furniture selection, meeting rooms, executive cabins, collaboration zones, and interior finishes, we create a workplace that reflects your organization and supports the way your team works.

MOFIS offers flexible managed office solutions that can accommodate businesses of varying sizes — from growing startups to large enterprises. Whether you require a compact office for a small team or a dedicated workspace for several hundred employees, we design and deliver offices that align with your current needs while allowing room for future expansion.

Every MOFIS office is delivered as a complete, move-in-ready workspace with everything your business needs to operate from day one. Our managed office solutions typically include:

  • Fully furnished workstations and private cabins
  • Custom office interiors
  • High-speed internet and enterprise-grade IT infrastructure
  • Meeting and conference rooms
  • Reception and visitor management
  • Pantry and breakout spaces
  • Housekeeping and facility management
  • 24×7 security and access control
  • Power backup and utilities
  • Maintenance and operational support

Our goal is to ensure your team can focus entirely on business while we take care of the workplace.

MOFIS is designed for businesses that want a professional, fully managed workplace without the time, cost, and complexity of setting up and operating an office independently. Our solutions are ideal for:

  • Startups
  • Growing businesses
  • SMEs
  • Scale-ups
  • Enterprise organizations
  • Global Capability Centres (GCCs)
  • IT & technology companies
  • Consulting firms
  • Creative agencies
  • Businesses expanding into new markets

Whether you're establishing your first office or expanding across multiple locations, MOFIS provides a workspace that adapts to your business.

Once the agreement is signed and the workspace has been finalized, the complete office setup typically takes a minimum of 60–90 days. During this period, our team manages every stage of the process — including design, construction, interior fit-out, furniture installation, technology integration, and final handover — ensuring your office is fully operational and ready for your team to move in.
